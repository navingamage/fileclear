What it does

Six obligations, four clocks, two governments.

That is what an owner managed corporation carries, and no single place tells you which of them are yours.

It asks about your company first

Where you incorporated, when, your year end, how you are registered for HST, whether you run payroll. Those answers change the plan, not the wording.

Then it builds your calendar

A federal corporation files its annual return 60 days after the anniversary of incorporation. An Ontario one files with the province six months after year end. You get the one you owe.

And it produces the numbers

HST returns, year end financials, the figures each form asks for. Ready to enter or to hand to an accountant.

Who it is for

Small corporations that do not need a full time accountant.

One or two people, a single revenue stream, a year end that arrives faster than expected. If your books are a spreadsheet and a shoebox, this is aimed at you.

The one everyone misses: since 2021 the Ontario annual return goes to the province through its own registry, not to CRA with your T2. Corporations with a perfectly filed return sit in default because nobody owns that one.
