No connection

FileClear cannot reach the server.

Every deadline in this product is worked out when you look at it rather than stored, so that a corrected rule reaches you the next morning instead of the next time you reinstall. The cost of that is this screen: with no connection there is nothing to compute from, and showing you a cached calendar would mean showing you one that might already be wrong.

Your reminders are unaffected. They are sent from the server on its own schedule and do not depend on this app being open, or even installed.
